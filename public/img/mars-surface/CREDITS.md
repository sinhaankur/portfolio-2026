# Mars surface imagery — provenance

Real, public-domain photographs taken **on the Martian surface** by NASA rovers,
sourced from the NASA Image and Video Library (images.nasa.gov). Used in the
`/lab/celestial` Mars coverage map. These are genuine surface photos — NOT
artist renders — so the "how little of Mars is actually imaged" story stays
honest.

| File | NASA ID | What it is |
|------|---------|-----------|
| perseverance.webp | PIA24264 | Perseverance Mastcam-Z first 360° panorama, Jezero Crater |
| curiosity.webp | PIA19803 | Curiosity Mastcam view of Martian geology, Gale Crater / Mt Sharp |
| opportunity.webp | PIA22074 | Opportunity view from within 'Perseverance Valley', Endeavour Crater |

Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech (and MSSS/ASU for Mastcam). Public domain.

Global maps (`public/textures/mars-4k.webp`, `mars-mola.webp`) are NASA MOLA
topography + color, downscaled from the celestial-assets masters.
